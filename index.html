01 — About

The name is a promise.

Falak (فلك) is the old word for the celestial sphere — the turning vault of the sky that astronomers have studied for a thousand years. 1259 marks the year the great Maragheh observatory opened its doors, pairing careful observation with rigorous computation for the first time.

We carry that spirit forward. Falak 1259 blends astrophysics with data science to make the universe legible — building open pipelines, reproducible models, and tools that let anyone ask a question of the sky and get an honest answer back.

02 — Missions

What we work on.

🔭

Sky Surveys

Ingesting terabytes of imaging from ground and space telescopes, cleaning, and cross-matching against open catalogues.

🌌

Galaxy Classification

Deep-learning models that sort spirals, ellipticals, and mergers — with calibrated uncertainty on every label.

☄️

Orbit Prediction

Physics-informed forecasting of asteroid and comet trajectories to flag close approaches weeks in advance.

📡

Transient Alerts

Real-time detection of supernovae and variable stars from streaming photometry, ranked by scientific interest.

🧪

Open Pipelines

Reproducible, well-documented workflows so any result we publish can be re-run from raw data to figure.

📖

Public Outreach

Notebooks, talks, and visualisations that bring the night sky — and the science behind it — to a wider audience.
