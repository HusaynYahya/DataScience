Mushāraṭa · Murāqaba · Muḥāsaba

The daily account

A day held between two conversations with yourself: a pledge in the morning setting the terms, watchfulness through the hours, and an accounting at night. The three are treated as one practice in the classical works on akhlāq — al-Ghazālī gives them a book of the Iḥyāʾ to themselves — and they only work together. A pledge with no reckoning is a wish; a reckoning with no pledge has nothing to measure.

The three

—
Mushāraṭa
Morning · the pledge

Stipulate the terms with yourself before the day starts. Name the one thing you will hold to and the one thing you will keep away from.

Murāqaba
Through the day · watchfulness

Sitting with the awareness that you are seen. Two short sittings beat one long one, and the minutes matter less than the fact that they happened.

Muḥāsaba
Night · the reckoning

Call yourself to account before you are called. Honest, brief, and written down — memory flatters, paper doesn't.

How the numbers are worked out

Body fat, when you don't enter one, is the US Navy circumference estimate: 495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077·log₁₀(waist − neck) + 0.15456·log₁₀(height)) − 450, in centimetres. It lands within about three to four percentage points of a DEXA scan — good enough to track a direction, not good enough to argue about a single reading. Waist at the navel, neck just below the larynx, both relaxed.

Lean mass is bodyweight minus fat mass, so it inherits that error. In a lean gain you want the lean line climbing and the fat line close to flat; if fat is rising faster than lean, the surplus is too big. Waist-to-height below 0.5 is the usual health marker — and the waist is the honest measure here, because it moves before the scale says anything useful.
