Psyche

Something got you, and it was bigger than it should have been.

Describe one reaction — what happened, and what it did to you. Then answer seven questions about it. At the end you get a reading in Jungian terms, the evidence it rests on, and something to actually do.

This is a reflective instrument, not therapy and not a diagnosis. It has no idea who you are and only your own account to go on.

About this

What it is doing

Jung's complexes are feeling-toned clusters in the unconscious that behave autonomously. When one is constellated it takes the wheel, and the giveaway is not the content of the reaction but its size: the affect does not match the stimulus. So this page never asks whether you were right. It asks how big the reaction was against the event, how old you felt, how familiar the feeling is — and then whichever further questions best separate the readings still in contention.

Twelve dynamics are considered: shadow projection, persona threat, the inferior function, the mother and father complexes, the abandoned child, puer and senex, anima and animus possession, the rescuer, the group's shadow, the unlived life, and the power complex. Each is scored against the signals your answers produce, and each reading is shown with the evidence that carried it, so you can disagree with it specifically.

What it is not doing

There is no model here, no server and no cleverness — it is a rule engine you could read in an afternoon (engine.js, with its tests in test/engine.test.js). It cannot see you, it has only the account you typed, and a reading that does not land is simply wrong. Treat the output as a set of questions worth asking yourself, which is what it is, and not as a finding.

It is not therapy, not analysis, and not a diagnosis of anything. If a pattern is costing you relationships, work or sleep, that is the point at which it wants a person rather than a page.

Where your writing goes

Nowhere. Everything runs in this browser: no network requests, no analytics, no accounts. Saving a reading puts it in this browser's local storage on this device, and deleting it removes it. Clearing your browser data removes it too.
